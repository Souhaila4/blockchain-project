LATEST DECISION: MENUS, PURCHASES AND REWARDS (read this first, it replaces the "send points" idea)

What changed in one sentence
Restaurants no longer send points to chosen wallets. Each restaurant publishes a MENU (item, price, points earned) and a list of REWARDS (free coffee, sandwich..., points needed). A customer BUYS an item with the wallet, the money goes to the restaurant, and the points are created for the customer automatically. Points are then spent on the rewards.

Everything below that talks about send points / mint / token.redeem / "Send points" button / AwardPointsForm / the free "spend points" form is OLD. The ERC-20 tokens, the acceptance rules and the dashboard layout are still valid.

Contracts (Solidity 0.8.20, 58 tests passing with "truffle test")
- RestaurantMarket.sol (NEW): menus, rewards, purchase and claim. It never holds money. It has no owner and no admin.
- RestaurantToken.sol (CHANGED): only the market can create ("mint") or burn points now. The restaurant wallet can no longer mint by hand. New function redeem(customer, atRestaurant, points) used by the market, still emits Redeemed. New error NotMarket.
- RestaurantRegistry.sol (CHANGED): it deploys the market in its own constructor (so there is nothing else to deploy and nothing to configure), gives it to every token it creates, and has a new market() getter and tokenOf(address) -> (token, isActive).
- interfaces/IRestaurantToken.sol (NEW). test/mocks/HostileRestaurant.sol (NEW, tests only: a restaurant wallet that refuses money or tries to re-enter).

Market functions
- Restaurant owner: addItem(name, price, points), updateItem(itemId, price, points, isActive), addReward(name, cost), updateReward(rewardId, cost, isActive). Items and rewards are never deleted, only hidden (isActive = false), so old ids and old history always keep their meaning. Max 50 items and 50 rewards per restaurant, names up to 64 bytes, price and cost must be above zero (an item may earn 0 points).
- Customer: purchase(restaurant, itemId, quantity) payable. The wallet must send EXACTLY price x quantity. The contract creates points x quantity for the buyer, then forwards the money to the restaurant wallet. If the restaurant wallet refuses the money the whole purchase is cancelled. A restaurant cannot buy from itself, and cannot sell while paused.
- Customer: claimReward(restaurant, rewardId, token). It burns "cost" points of the chosen token. The restaurant must be active and must accept that token (own token only / all tokens / selected restaurants, same rules as before). A customer may pay with another restaurant's token if the restaurant accepts it.
- Views: getItems(restaurant), getRewards(restaurant). Events: ItemAdded/ItemUpdated, RewardAdded/RewardUpdated, Purchased(restaurant, customer, itemId, quantity, paid, points), RewardClaimed(restaurant, customer, rewardId, token, points). The pages read history from these events.
- Payment currency is the chain's native coin (ETH on Ganache), prices are stored in wei. 1 point is a whole number (token has 0 decimals).

Own restaurant shows a note instead of Buy buttons, a paused one shows a warning.
- Changed: the sidebar (owners get "Menu & Rewards"), the restaurant cards (they open the restaurant page), the owner dashboard ("Total Points Awarded" now means points earned by customers from purchases, new "Total sales" row, "Edit menu & rewards" button), the customer dashboard, Transactions (purchases and reward claims with item or reward name, price paid, points +/-), Points & Rewards (read only: points you can spend and already spent).

- New lib files: abi.ts, chain.ts (provider/contract helpers), market.ts, activity.ts. contracts.ts now only does registry and tokens. validation.ts and errors.ts know the new rules and messages (wrong payment, paused restaurant, token not accepted, hidden item...).

Things to know / risks
- Money goes straight from the customer to the restaurant wallet inside purchase(); there is no refund function and no platform fee.
- A restaurant could buy from ANOTHER wallet it controls to farm points for free. The self-purchase block is not protection against that. Points only have value where a restaurant accepts them, which is why "Tokens of all restaurants" is risky: prefer "restaurants I work with".

- Prices are in ETH, not in a stable coin. A real launch would need a stable token or a price feed.

ERC-20 tokens per restaurant (CURRENT)

Why a new contract and not the old ones
An ERC-20 token is one contract = one token. Per-restaurant tokens therefore need a token contract that is deployed once per restaurant. We did not put this logic inside the registry, and we did not keep LoyaltyPoints (one shared balance cannot be per-restaurant). So:
- RestaurantToken.sol (NEW): the ERC-20 token. One is deployed for every restaurant.
- RestaurantRegistry.sol (CHANGED): it is now also the factory. registerRestaurant deploys the restaurant's token and stores the acceptance rules.
- interfaces/IRestaurantRegistry.sol (NEW): the 2 functions the token asks the registry (isAuthorizedRestaurant, acceptsToken).
- LoyaltyPoints.sol, its migration and its test: DELETED. Replaced by the tokens.
- We use OpenZeppelin ERC20 (version 5.0.2, pinned, in package.json at the project root). Run "npm install" in the project root once. It is the audited standard, so we did not write an ERC-20 by hand.

Current address (Ganache, chain id 1337, http://127.0.0.1:7545) - fresh and empty
- RestaurantRegistry: 0x93AE37d46287A4FE71bD52aC89aB5EE003629DFa (block 153)
- There is no second contract to deploy any more. Tokens are created by the registry when a restaurant is created.
- Address and block are in loyalfront/lib/config.ts. After every "truffle migrate --reset" update REGISTRY_ADDRESS and REGISTRY_DEPLOY_BLOCK there.

How it works
1. A wallet creates its restaurant: registerRestaurant(name, location, symbol, mode, selectedPartners). The registry deploys a token called "<name> Points" with your symbol (example: "Cafe Luna Points", LUNA). The restaurant wallet is the only wallet that can ever mint that token.
2. When creating, the owner chooses which tokens the restaurant accepts as payment (its own token is always accepted):
   - Only my own token (OwnTokenOnly)
   - Tokens of all restaurants (AllTokens), including restaurants created later
   - Tokens of restaurants I work with (SelectedPartners): only the restaurants ticked in the list
   It can be changed later: setAcceptanceMode(mode) and setPartner(restaurant, true/false).
3. A restaurant sends points by minting its token to a wallet: token.mint(wallet, points).
4. A customer spends points with token.redeem(restaurant, points). The contract checks that this restaurant is active and accepts this token (registry.acceptsToken), then BURNS the points, adds them to the customer's "spent" total and emits Redeemed(customer, restaurant, points). The Redeemed event says which restaurant honored the points, so restaurants can settle between themselves outside the chain.
5. Tokens are normal ERC-20: they can be transferred between wallets and shown in MetaMask. They have 0 decimals (whole points only).

Rules and protections
- Only the restaurant that owns a token can mint it (NotIssuer). The deployer has no power over any restaurant.
- A paused restaurant cannot mint and accepts no token. Customers can still spend their points at other restaurants that accept them.
- Acceptance is one way: if Luna lists Nori, Luna accepts NORI tokens, but Nori does not automatically accept LUNA.
- A restaurant cannot list itself, and can only list restaurants that exist. Zero amounts are rejected. Spending more than you hold is rejected by the ERC-20 itself.
- Tokens spent are burned, so totalSupply of a token = points still in customers' hands.

Contract functions
RestaurantRegistry: registerRestaurant(name, location, symbol, mode, selectedPartners), setRestaurantStatus(isActive), setAcceptanceMode(mode), setPartner(partner, accepted), restaurants(address), partners(restaurant, partner), issuerOf(token), isAuthorizedRestaurant(address), acceptsToken(restaurant, token). Event RestaurantRegistered is how the app finds every restaurant (and its token). Events AcceptanceModeUpdated, PartnerUpdated, RestaurantStatusUpdated.
RestaurantToken: mint(to, points), redeem(atRestaurant, points), redeemedOf(customer), plus the ERC-20 functions (balanceOf, transfer, approve, name, symbol, totalSupply...).

What you see in the app (loyalfront): see "Dashboard and navigation" above. The same functions are used (create restaurant with symbol and accepted tokens, send points = mint, change accepted tokens, pause / resume, spend points = redeem), now on their own pages instead of popups.

RestaurantRegistry review (first version, owner-based)

The contract lets the platform owner register restaurants and turn them active or inactive. A restaurant is authorized only when it is both registered and active.

What we changed:
- Custom errors instead of revert strings
- Reject zero address, empty name, empty location, and oversized strings (name 128 bytes, location 256 bytes)
- Skip a status update when the value is already stored
- Two-step ownership transfer so the owner key can be rotated. No renounce, so registration cannot be frozen by accident
- Packed the two flags in one storage slot and read them once in isAuthorizedRestaurant
- String arguments use calldata. Compiler optimizer is on

Registration rules are the same: owner-only, new restaurants start active, the same address cannot be registered twice, and name and location stay fixed after registration.
